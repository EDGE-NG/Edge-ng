Questions, answered.

What is Edge?

A tool that turns your trading strategy into a checklist and reviews each setup against it before you trade.

Does Edge tell me what to trade?

No. It gives no signals or predictions. You choose every trade.

What if the chart isn’t clear?

Edge marks the rule Inconclusive and asks you for another timeframe or view.

Can I trade against a Fail?

Yes. Edge records the override and shows it in your journal.

Does Edge place trades or connect to my broker?

Not at launch. You record trades yourself. Connections may come later.

Is this financial advice?

No. Edge is decision support. Trading involves risk.
